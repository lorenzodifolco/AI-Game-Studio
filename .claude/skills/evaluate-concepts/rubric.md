# Concept Rubric

## Hard gates (any FAIL → `rejected`, no score needed)
| Gate | Fails if |
|---|---|
| Originality / IP | Recreates a specific existing game (name, characters, art, levels, distinctive look) or uses trademarks. Genre and mechanic similarity is fine. |
| Brief compliance | Violates `studio/brief.md` (no-go list, platform, audience, scope limits). |
| Feasibility | Needs tech or content the studio can't make: 3D, realtime multiplayer, licensed IP, heavy hand-drawn art, live-ops at launch. |
| Policy | Likely Google Play policy trouble (real-money gambling, deceptive ads, user-generated content without moderation, kids-directed without a Families plan). |

## Weighted criteria (score 1–10)
| Criterion | Weight | 10 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Market demand | 20% | Growing subgenre with clear evidence in the research report |
| Differentiation | 20% | A hook you can see in a 3-second clip; no close equivalent in the top charts |
| Core-loop strength | 20% | A satisfying 5-second action and a clear session goal; easy to learn, room for mastery |
| Retention / meta | 10% | Credible reasons to return on D1, D7 and D30 |
| Monetization fit | 10% | A natural, player-friendly model with proven benchmarks |
| Feasibility / scope | 15% | Prototype ≤ 1 week, production ≤ the brief's limit, art achievable with SVG or code-drawn shapes |
| Organic marketability | 5% | Store page and short video sell themselves; shareable moments |

**Total** = Σ score × weight (1 decimal). **Shortlist threshold: 7.0**, with every hard gate passing. Shortlist at most 3; prefer diversity of risk.

## Evaluation section format (append to each concept)
```
## Evaluation
- Hard gates: Originality PASS · Brief PASS · Feasibility PASS · Policy PASS
- Closest existing games: <names (store links)>; clone risk: low/med/high
- Scores: Demand 7 · Diff 8 · Loop 7 · Retention 6 · Monet 7 · Feas 8 · Organic 7 → **7.3**
- Strongest case for: ...
- Strongest case against (pre-mortem): ...
- Required changes if greenlit: ...
- Decision: shortlisted | rejected | parked (reason)
```
