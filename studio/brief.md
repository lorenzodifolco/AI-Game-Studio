# Studio Brief (charter)

The owner edits this file. Research, ideation and evaluation agents treat it as binding.
Sections marked ✏️ are defaults you should review.

## Mission
Ship small, polished, original Android games that find an audience organically, then double
down on what works. Optimize for learning speed: many cheap prototypes, few expensive productions.

## Team and capacity ✏️
- An AI studio (Claude Code agents) plus one human owner who approves gates, playtests, signs and uploads.
- One active game at a time (`studio/config.json → max_active_games`).
- Prototype ≤ 1 week of AI-dev effort. Production ≤ 4 weeks. Content must be producible with code-drawn or SVG art.

## Platform and tech
- Android first (Google Play), phones, **portrait**, **offline-first**, Godot 4 (GL Compatibility).
- No backend at launch: no accounts, multiplayer, or server-side live-ops.

## Audience ✏️
- General audience 13+. Not directed at children (avoids Families-policy and COPPA scope at this stage).
- Casual and mid-core players who play in 2–10 minute sessions.

## Business model ✏️
- Free-to-play with player-friendly monetization: opt-in rewarded ads, plus a single "remove ads / supporter" IAP. Premium is acceptable where the research supports it.
- Marketing budget: **zero paid UA** at first. Store page, ASO, short-form video and communities only, so the hook must be visible in 3 seconds.

## Genre preferences ✏️
- Open. Research decides. Favour genres where game feel and a clever mechanic beat content volume (puzzle, arcade, hyper/hybrid-casual, idle/incremental, roguelite-lite, physics toys).

## No-go list
- Clones or recreations of specific existing games, their names, characters, art or levels.
- Real-money gambling or casino mechanics, loot boxes sold for money, deceptive ads or dark patterns.
- User-generated content or chat (moderation burden), licensed IP, 3D-heavy or narrative-heavy content.
- Hateful, sexual, or extreme-violence content.

## Success metrics ✏️
- Prototype: the kill criteria in each concept are met, and the human playtester wants "one more round".
- Launch (first 30 days): D1 ≥ 30%, D7 ≥ 10%, rating ≥ 4.2. Use these to decide whether to iterate or move on.
